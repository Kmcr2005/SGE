About CELESTIA '26

CELESTIA ’26 is the flagship space and geospatial event organized by the Society of Geoinformatics Engineers (SGE), Institute of Remote Sensing, Anna University, Chennai, bringing together students, researchers, and enthusiasts to explore the fascinating intersection of space science, remote sensing, geospatial technology, and innovation.

With the theme “Geovistas: Decoding Space for Innovation, Integration & Leadership,” CELESTIA ’26 provides a dynamic platform for participants to learn, compete, collaborate, and transform ideas into impactful solutions.

The two-day event features a diverse range of technical and non-technical workshops, competitions, interactive sessions, and engaging activities, covering emerging areas of space science, planetary exploration, remote sensing, GIS, and geospatial technologies.

CELESTIA ’26 aims to inspire the next generation of geospatial professionals, space enthusiasts, innovators, and leaders by fostering curiosity, creativity, technical excellence, and collaboration.
